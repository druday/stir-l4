| **Asian people.** Results for Asian participants described apart. | your approved question |
| **Reporting.** Scoping part with PRISMA-ScR, systematic part with PRISMA 2020: reporting checklists for each kind (#9, #10) (my suggestion). | [16] |
| **Limitations.** One person screens, reads and judges. One reviewer's full-text screening missed relevant studies in a 2026 study (#2); raters agreed only slightly on overall risk of bias in 2020 (#3). | you; [16] |
