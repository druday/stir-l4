By a declared rule over the review records examined during planning (type 2 diabetes named as the population, muscle or lean mass named, and a systematic review or meta-analysis by its title; record: L000771), 6 reviews were found; none names adults without diabetes. In a meta-analysis of 8 randomized trials with 541 participants (its title names older adults) [8] (2026), fat mass fell (standardized mean difference −0.53); muscle mass fell less (standardized mean difference −0.35), which the authors call substantially smaller than the fat loss. In a network meta-analysis of 33 randomized trials with 5,399 participants [9] (2026), fat mass fell consistently (for example dapagliflozin −1.75 kg); lean mass fell modestly with empagliflozin (−1.22 kg), ipragliflozin (−0.99 kg) and canagliflozin (−0.80 kg) but not with dapagliflozin or tofogliflozin, and no SGLT2 inhibitor significantly reduced skeletal muscle mass compared with placebo; strength and physical performance were rarely reported. In a meta-analysis of 5 randomized trials of at least 12 months with 695 participants [10] (2026), fat mass fell (−1.26 kg); fat-free mass did not differ significantly (−0.53 kg; 95% confidence interval −1.62 to 0.56). In a meta-analysis of 19 randomized trials with 1,482 participants [11] (2023), fat is not reported in the abstract; lean body mass fell by 0.66 and skeletal muscle mass by 0.35 (units not given); the quality of the evidence was low. In a meta-analysis of 25 randomized trials with 2,286 participants [12] (2023), fat mass fell (−1.49); lean mass (−0.80), skeletal muscle mass (−0.38) and skeletal muscle index (−0.12) fell as well; body water fell (−0.96); units are not given in the abstract. In a meta-analysis of 18 studies with 1,430 participants [13] (2022), fat mass fell (−1.16 kg); lean mass changed by −0.76 kg (95% confidence interval −1.53 to 0.01) and skeletal muscle mass fell (−1.01 kg). Item 15a. Criteria for quantitative synthesis

A part of the weight is pooled in a meta-analysis only if 3 or more similar randomized trials report it; cohort studies are not counted toward the 3 (certified; record: L000655). The scoping part covers every study that meets the question. Where a part of the weight is not pooled, results are tabled and described part by part and reported with SWiM [30] (certified; record: L000655).

Item 16. Meta-bias(es)

For each trial, the outcomes in its register entry are compared with those it reports (my suggestion). No statistical test for publication bias is planned, as few trials are expected (my suggestion).

Item 17. Confidence in cumulative evidence

The certainty of the evidence for each part of the weight is rated with GRADE (certified), following the Core GRADE guidance on risk of bias and publication bias [31].

Reporting and limitations (after item 17)

The scoping part will be reported with PRISMA-ScR [32] and the systematic part with PRISMA 2020 [33] (certified). This protocol follows PRISMA-P 2015 [34], whose authors encourage its use, as applicable, for any type of review protocol, and its searches follow PRISMA-S [14]. Limitations (certified): one person screens, reads and judges; the assistant's suggestions are not checked by a second person; and the author of the prompt and kit uses them and judges their suggestions alone.
