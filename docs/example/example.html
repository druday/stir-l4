STIR-L4 · prompt version 1.1 · helper kit 1.1.4

A worked example

One simulated planning session, shown exactly as the assistant replied

STIR-L4 represents the planning of a systematic review as ten elements joined by 27 flows. An AI assistant retrieves facts at five decisions through logged searches and drafts text, and a named reviewer certifies every decision. Version 1.1 of the copy-paste prompt keeps the theory unchanged and changes how the assistant talks with the team: every reply shows where the team is, what just happened and what comes next, the research focus is asked for and carried through, searches are shown in full, and nothing has to be opened in a file.

About this example

  • User: a simulated solo reviewer, Uday, played by a second AI from a persona card modeled on the first live test. The persona is terse, picks from lists, replies "Certify" without a reason and says "just start the review process".
  • Topic: SGLT2 inhibitors for weight management in non-diabetic Asian populations.
  • Assistant: an AI assistant (Claude) given only the STIR-L4 prompt v1.1 and the files a team would upload: the prompt, the helper kit, the model file and the core-properties file.
  • Searches: real. PubMed and OpenAlex were searched live on 25 October 2026 (ClinicalTrials.gov was tested at set-up), and every response was saved and logged.
  • Before planning: the first-turn tool test and the 16-question calibration quiz (scored 16 of 16) are not shown.
  • Fidelity: the conversation is reproduced word for word. Only the layout and the numbered notes in the margin are added.

What to look for

  1. 1The status line on every reply
  2. 2Done, Next and Your turn, in the same order every time
  3. 3The plan map and quick commands
  4. 4The research focus asked for, reflected back and carried forward
  5. 5Certification in plain words
  6. 6"Just start" handled without skipping a step
  7. 7Searches shown in full, every number tied to a logged query
  8. 8Recaps and "what's next" on request
  9. 9Pause: summary in the chat, workspace packaged
